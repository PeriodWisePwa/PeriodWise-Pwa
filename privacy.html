Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [25th March,2026]

PeriodWise is a privacy-first application designed to help users track their menstrual cycle and related health information.

1. Data Collection

PeriodWise does not collect, store, or share any personal data within the app.

No account registration is required, and no personal information such as email addresses is collected directly by the app.

2. Local Data Storage

All data entered into the app (such as cycle tracking information) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

3. Internet Usage

Some features of the app, such as educational articles, may require an internet connection. These features may open external web pages.

4. Third-Party Services

PeriodWise does not use third-party services to collect personal data within the app itself.

However, external web pages (such as our blog) that are accessible through the app may include features like email subscription forms or analytics tools. These services may collect limited user information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Users are advised to review the privacy policies of those external pages before submitting any personal information.

5. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

6. Children's Privacy

PeriodWise does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.
